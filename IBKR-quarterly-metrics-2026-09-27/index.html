Volume / activity

The franchise is still compounding. Accounts ended Q2’26 at 5.185M, up from 3.866M a year earlier (~+34%). DARTs were 4.824M versus 3.552M (~+36%). Customer equity — franchise scale color, not the toll itself — printed $930.3B versus $664.6B.

Post-2023 is the right window for this panel: that is where accounts, DARTs, and the issuer’s $/order print sit together densely. Pre-2023 DARTs are blank in this extract and stay out of the chart.

Activity rose with the account base. DARTs per account edged to ~0.930 from ~0.919. That is more volume on a larger franchise — not a hollow print.

Monetization

The fee toll grew in dollars. Commissions $673M versus $516M (+30.4% nominal; +26.1% in 2026-06 CPI dollars). Other fees and services $87M versus $62M. Net revenues $1,896M versus $1,480M; pretax $1,456M versus $1,104M. Pretax margin held around 76.8% — the honesty panel beside the toll, never stacked with it.

Unit price did not collapse. Commission per cleared commissionable order was $2.64 versus $2.65 a year ago — a gentle glide in the mid-$2s while activity scaled. Commissions per account eased modestly (~$129.80 versus ~$133.47). The dollar split is blunt: commissions rose +$157M year over year; account growth contributed about +$176M; softer commissions per account took back about −$19M. Accounts carried the print. Intensity was a modest drag. That is an identity split, not a causal proof — but it is the right shape for a scaling franchise with a slightly easier unit take.

Quarterly fee yield on client equity was ~7.23 bps. Equity grew faster than commission dollars, so yield compressed even as absolute toll dollars rose. Equity growth includes market moves — a caveat, not a beta attribution this cut.

Mix that explains the rate

Inside the contract-revenue note, commissions still dominate. Q2’26 mix: commissions $673M, market data $23M, risk exposure $25M, payment for order flow $18M, FDIC sweep $10M, other fees $11M. Commissions are roughly 89% of that disclosed fee stack. The non-commission lines sum to $87M — the same dollars as the income-statement “other fees and services” line this quarter. Minimum-activity fees are not printed here. Do not invent an API or Lite attach rate from payment for order flow or the FDIC sweep.

Management geography: US net revenues $1,284M, international $612M — international about 32% of the US plus international total. Near one-third international is supportive of the global franchise, not a stall print. Empty first-quarter geography stubs stay blank; they are not filled in. Commission dollars by geography remain blank — do not dress consolidated commissions up as a regional fee toll.
