Monetization — take intensity and relative quality

10.1 (FY2024) → mid-10s through mid-2025 → 8.5 (FY2025) → 8.7 / 8.8 (Q1–Q2’26). The industry total-transaction-value source switches to NAR figures beginning in the fourth-quarter 2025 window — slope and method change overlap.

For Sale Q2’26 $549M; Rentals $209M (versus $159M a year earlier). For Sale is the core wallet; Rentals is the compounding second engine on the same front door. Q1–Q3’23 For Sale chart points are Residential plus Mortgages, marked derived.

Relative uplift (not a dollar figure per connection): Preferred generated +23% more revenue per connection than the legacy advertising model in 2025; outlook +35% by end-2026. That is pricing and conversion quality of the mix shift — not a disclosed dollars-per-connection series.

Anti-pattern: For Sale dollars times Preferred percentage is not Preferred revenue. Attachment is share of connections, not share of dollars.

What we will not invent

Absolute connection count N is a gap. Dollars per connection is a gap as fact. The sensitivity table shows ceilings under explicit assumed N (using full-year 2025 For Sale as a contaminated upper bound) — assumption only. Preferred segment dollars and fee percentage of commission stay blank.

At attachment \(m\) and uplift \(R\), Preferred’s share of connection revenue is \(m(1+R)/(1+mR)\) — derived shape math, still not For Sale times \(m\). At m=49% and R=23% that is roughly 54% of connection revenue; at m=75% and R=35% roughly 80%. Mix understates Preferred’s economic weight inside the connection pool.
