Spotify — key metrics, Q2’26

SPOT · EUR · series 2026-09-16T19:00:00Z · built 2026-09-16 19:53 UTC

The short version

Spotify closed Q2’26 with 777M monthly users, 300M Premium subscribers and 494M ad-supported MAUs. Revenue was €4.78B (+14% YoY reported), operating margin 13.7%, free cash flow €797M — about €3.26B over the trailing four quarters. The stock trades at roughly 33× trailing earnings; street forward prints sit near 43× (FY1) and 34× (FY2).

Long-only, 3–10 year horizon. Primary falsifier: MAU / engagement slowdown. Secondary: a sustained quarter-over-quarter operating-margin decline. Moat is distribution, product and scale — not music rights.

Users — still compounding in an 11–12% band

Growth at Q2’26: MAU +11.6%, Premium +8.7%, Ad-Supported +14.1% YoY. MAU has sat in an ~11–12% YoY band for eight quarters — neither a break nor a re-acceleration. Premium growth has cooled from ~12% in H1’25 while Ad runs faster at the top of the funnel. That wedge is the live tension for the thesis, and it is why the primary falsifier is MAU / engagement rather than ARPU.

Net adds stay seasonal: Q4 spikes, Q1 soft. Q2’26 added +16M MAU (a touch below the +18M of Q2’25), +7M Premium (the recent mid-single-millions run-rate) and +11M Ad. Premium ÷ MAU reads ~38.6% — that is stock mix, not conversion; the true free→paid rate is undisclosed, and the conversion dig lives on its own page.

Monetization — price is carrying Premium

Premium ARPU printed €4.89 in Q2’26, up from €4.76 in Q1 and €4.57 a year ago. Price and mix are doing the work on the paid side while subscriber growth decelerates. Premium revenue was €4.33B against €446M for Ad — Premium is still the profit engine; Ad is the scale and funnel story at a much smaller absolute line.

One gap worth naming: podcast and audiobook hours / share are null across the entire content KPI file, so IR format claims (Audiobooks+ ARR, penetration) stay narrative until Product lands a stable unit.

P&L and cash — margin expansion is real on a FY basis

Operating margin stepped down from 15.8% in Q1’26 to 13.7% in Q2 — one sequential step after a strong Q1, not yet the sustained decline the secondary falsifier watches for. On a full-year basis the expansion is real: 8.7% in 2024 to 12.8% in 2025, with net income durably positive after the 2022–23 loss years (FY2025 net income €2.21B).

Free cash flow has graduated from thin to multi-billion: €797M in Q2’26, €2.87B in FY2025 versus €2.29B in FY2024, and roughly €3.26B across the trailing four quarters.

Method & definitional notes
  • Premium and Ad-Supported MAU are overlapping cohorts — never summed to total MAU.
  • Premium ÷ MAU (~38.6% at Q2’26) is stock mix, not a free→paid conversion rate; true conversion is undisclosed and lives in the separate conversion pack.
  • Q4’24–Q4’25 revenue / operating income (and Q4’18–Q4’20 revenue / gross profit / segment revenue) are derived FY − Q1−Q3 and flagged in the series; fine for trajectory, not new accession facts.
  • Q4 Premium ARPU is blank by rule: FY stubs carry a year-average ARPU, not a Q4 print.
  • All PE prints are mixed units — USD price over EUR earnings, never FX-adjusted.
  • pe_ttm prints only where trailing-four-quarter net income is complete and positive; sparse markers, never connected or interpolated. pe_fy is FY-end only.
  • Weekly trailing PE uses complete ISO weeks only; price is a volume-weighted average of daily closes (not a true VWAP).
  • Forward PE is the single hybrid official print (Yahoo non-GAAP EUR EPS × weekly USD price, asof 2026-09-16); pre-snapshot history is null by design and NTM is null until all four quarter estimates exist.
  • Free cash flow is the cabinet non-IFRS reconciliation; Q4 values are FY − Q1−Q3 residuals.
  • Podcast / audiobook hours and share are null across the content KPI file — IR format claims stay narrative.
  • 50 period parse-warnings in the file (11 Q4/residual-related). Residuals already on series.json are respected; this builder does not recompute them. Examples: label FY2018 -> FY2018Q4 (year-end row is the Q4 print); q4_residual_fy_minus_q1q2q3 (semi-annual era; flagged); label FY2019 -> FY2019Q4 (year-end row is the Q4 print); label FY2020 -> FY2020Q4 (year-end row is the Q4 print).
  • Non-additive: Premium MAU and Ad-Supported MAU are overlapping bases; they are not additive to MAU.
