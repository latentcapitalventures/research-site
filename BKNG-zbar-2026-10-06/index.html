Booking Holdings - key metrics pack from the desk series file. Latest print in file: FY2026Q3 (period end 2026-09-30).

Booking Desk · Latent capital · through the quarter ended September 30, 2026

Letter open

Booking Holdings runs Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, KAYAK, and OpenTable. It earns a commission when a trip is booked - sometimes collecting the traveler's payment itself (merchant), sometimes letting the hotel collect it (agency) - plus advertising fees.

This page tracks the scoreboard: profit, share count, earnings per share, and what the market pays for those earnings. Room nights, gross bookings, and take rate are on the unit-econ dig; the AI question is on the AI dig.

Major risks (≤4)

  • Travel demand and currency swings move earnings faster than the business itself changes.
  • Other travel sites, Google, and hotels selling direct compete for the same traveler.
  • The merchant-versus-agency mix moves reported revenue and cash differently from bookings.
  • AI assistants could sit between the traveler and Booking - covered on the AI dig.

Close comps (≤5)

  • Expedia - closest large online travel peer.
  • Airbnb - alternative stays, sold direct to guests.
  • Trip.com - major online travel agency, strongest in Asia.
  • Google Travel / Hotels - hotel search that competes for paid traffic.
  • Amadeus - travel booking systems; a different model, useful for distribution context.

Consolidated income

In 2025 revenue was $26.917B, operating income $8.825B, and net income $5.404B - up about 13% and 17% on 2024 for the first two, down about 8% for net income (DERIVED from printed levels).

Q3 2026 as reported: revenue $8.893B, operating income $3.418B, and net income $2.717B, each about 1-2% below Q3 2025 (DERIVED); diluted EPS was $3.61.

The quarter carries a $1.0B charge for the FTC matter: $900M off revenue and $100M in expenses. No settlement is agreed and the FTC may seek more; the amount is still unpaid. Without the $900M, revenue was $9.793B, about +9% on a year ago (the company's non-GAAP figure; growth DERIVED). The charts show the reported numbers.

Shares and trailing EPS

Booking keeps shrinking its share count. Q3 2026 diluted shares were 753M, about 7% fewer than a year earlier, after about $2.1B of buybacks in the quarter. All counts are restated for the 25-for-1 split.

Trailing EPS - the last four quarters of diluted EPS added up - is about $9.27 through Q3 2026 (CALCULATED: $1.7688 + $1.36 + $2.53 + $3.61), up from $9.04 a quarter earlier, with the FTC charge already in it.
