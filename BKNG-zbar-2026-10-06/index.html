Letter open

Booking Holdings is a global online travel company. Travelers use Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, KAYAK, and OpenTable to find and book stays and other travel. The company earns commissions and related fees when bookings complete - sometimes collecting payment itself (merchant), sometimes letting the supplier collect (agency) - plus advertising and metasearch fees. Brand-by-brand profit is not charted here.

Growth since the pandemic rebound has been led by the consolidated travel franchise. This pack locks income levels, diluted share count, trailing twelve-month diluted EPS, and trailing weekly PE. Room nights, gross bookings, and take rate live on the separate unit-econ dig ([live](https://latentcapitalventures.github.io/research-site/BKNG-unit-econ-2026-09-24/)). AI and distribution disintermediation live on the AI dig ([live](https://latentcapitalventures.github.io/research-site/BKNG-ai-disintermediation-2026-09-26/)). Forward PE stays a labeled gap until a locked NTM print exists.

Major risks (≤4)

  • Travel demand and FX can swing revenue and earnings faster than the operating franchise changes. Watch revenue, operating income, and net income on the charts below.
  • Online travel competition (other OTAs, Google Travel, supplier direct) can pressure conversion and mix without a separate brand P&L in this pack.
  • Merchant vs agency mix and payment timing can move reported revenue and cash differently from booking scale.
  • AI / distribution disintermediation is a live thesis question - handled on the AI dig, not this zbar.

Close comps (≤5)

  • Expedia - closest large OTA peer on stays and packages.
  • Airbnb - alternative accommodation and direct-to-guest overlap.
  • Trip.com - major international OTA, especially Asia.
  • Google Travel / Hotels - metasearch and free-listing pressure on paid acquisition.
  • Amadeus / Sabre - GDS / travel-tech adjacency (different model; distribution context).
