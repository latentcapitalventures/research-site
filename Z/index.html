Zillow — key metrics, Q2’26

Z · USD · series 2026-09-17T21:00:00Z · built 2026-09-17 20:41 UTC

The short version

Zillow’s Q2’26 was a hold-steady audience quarter with a growth engine underneath: 2,529M visits and 239M average monthly unique users, roughly flat to a year ago, while Rentals revenue grew ~31% and Zillow Home Loans origination nearly doubled. Revenue was $772M with $176M of Adj. EBITDA and a slightly negative GAAP net income (−$4M). The stock trades at roughly 130× trailing earnings — a multiple priced against a thin, just-turned-positive earnings base. Underneath the flat audience, attachment is scaling: Preferred / Enhanced connections reached 49% in Q1’26, up from 21% at the end of 2024, and issuer-quoted Comscore share held at 70% in both 2026 prints.

Long-only, 5–10 year horizon: the market overweights agentic-AI disruption versus Zillow’s marketplace and data moat. The primary falsifier is sustained audience decay; the secondary is AI-search taking a fast-rising share of housing queries. That is why the audience line leads this page.

Audience — flat, not broken

Q2’26 visits of 2,529M and 239M average monthly unique users sit just below Q2’25 (2,590M / 243M) — a low-single-digit drift, not a collapse. Q1 was softer at 2,276M / 220M against 2,354M / 227M a year earlier. Two years of quarterly prints show a plateau, not a downtrend break — exactly what the primary falsifier watches.

Full-year visit totals are annual sums (FY2025: 9,593M) and are labeled as such in the file; they never share an axis with the quarterly series.

Monetization — the TTV slope stepped down

For Sale revenue per industry total transaction value, on a trailing-twelve-month basis, ran 10.1–10.3 bps from FY2024 through 3Q25 — then stepped down to 8.5 bps at FY2025, 8.7 in 1Q26 and 8.8 in 2Q26. The last two prints tick back up, but the level reset from ~10 to the high-8s is the live monetization question: mix shift toward success-fee connections has to prove it can rebuild the slope from here.

Attachment — Preferred / Enhanced connections

The TTV reset has a mechanism underneath it: moving connections onto success-fee terms. Preferred / Enhanced connections were 21% of connections at the end of 2024 and reached 49% by Q1’26 — through 24, 27, 34 and 44 at the quarter-ends in between. The issuer’s program label shifted across those prints (Enhanced Markets vs Enhanced Market experience); the series is the same period-end % of connections throughout.

A newer, separate metric — integrated experience % of connections — printed 61% at Q2’26. It is a different definition, labeled here only so the two are not conflated; it is never merged into the Preferred / Enhanced series.

Growth engines — Rentals and ZHL

Rentals revenue reached $209M in Q2’26 versus $159M a year ago, about +31% — the clearest growth line in the stack. Zillow Home Loans purchase origination hit $2.17B versus $1.12B, nearly doubling year over year. Together they are the answer to a flat audience: more transaction per visit, not more visits.

P&L — supporting color

Revenue of $772M is up from $655M a year ago, with For Sale contributing $549M. Adj. EBITDA held at $176M versus $155M, while GAAP net income flipped slightly negative (−$4M versus +$2M) — margin quality intact, bottom line still thin. Cash and investments of $572M are down from $678M in 1Q26 and $1.08B at FY2024; diluted shares fell to 227.9M from 256.2M at 1Q25, so the buyback is doing real per-share work.

Method & definitional notes
  • FY annual visit totals are labeled annual and excluded from quarterly charts (FY2025 9,593M vs quarterly ~2.2–2.6B).
  • AMUU is null for FY2024Q1–Q2 — the line breaks there rather than interpolating.
  • pe_ttm prints only where trailing-four-quarter net income is complete and positive (1Q26, 2Q26); sparse labeled prints, never connected.
  • pe_fy is non-null only at FY2025 (≈753.90) and sits on thin earnings — FY2025 net income was only +$23M; it is a labeled print, not a trend point.
  • Weekly trailing PE uses complete ISO weeks only (VWAP of daily closes); positive-TTM coverage starts 2026-W14; latest ok week 2026-W37 ≈ 132.86.
  • FY2024Q4 / FY2025Q4 revenue and net income are FY − Q1−Q3 residuals (flagged on the rows); segment splits and Adj. EBITDA stay null.
  • Q2’26 pe_ttm uses the 2026-09-16 asof close ($31.32), not the quarter-end close.
  • Cash & investments mirrors cash & equivalents in the cabinet; short-term investments are null throughout.
  • Preferred / Enhanced % of connections is an issuer period-end print; the program label shifted between “Enhanced Markets” and “Enhanced Market experience” across prints, and the 2024-12-31 / 2025-12-31 reads sit on the cabinet’s FY rows.
  • Integrated experience % of connections (61% at 2026-06-30) is a separate metric (integrated_experience_pct_connections) — never merged into the Preferred / Enhanced series.
  • Comscore audience share is issuer-quoted (Zillow-cited), not a licensed Comscore series: 70% at both 2026-03-31 and 2026-06-30, group MUV cited once — two prints are a labeled table, never a share trend.
  • FY2024Q4: FY2024Q4 revenue / net income are FY2024 minus Q1−Q3 residuals (q4-residual:0001617640-25-000016); segment splits and Adj. EBITDA left null — not residualized.
  • FY2025Q4: FY2025Q4 revenue / net income are FY2025 minus Q1−Q3 residuals (q4-residual:0001617640-26-000015); segment splits and Adj. EBITDA left null — not residualized.
  • Non-additive: FY annual visit totals (FY2023 9,977M / FY2024 9,308M / FY2025 9,593M) are not comparable to quarterly visits (~2.2–2.6B) — never summed or charted on one axis.
