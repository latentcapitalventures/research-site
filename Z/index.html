The short version

Dear fellow investor — Zillow after Q2’26 is not primarily a traffic story. It is a marketplace that takes a small cut of industry home-sale dollars, and that is trying to earn a richer cut by moving more leads onto success-fee terms. For Sale revenue per industry TTV printed 8.8 bps on a trailing-twelve-month basis — up a tick from 8.7 in Q1’26, still below the ~10.1–10.3 band that held through FY24–3Q25, in a window that overlaps the TTV source flip to NAR. Preferred / Enhanced connections reached 49% by Q1’26, from 21% at the end of 2024, with management guiding past 75% Preferred by end-2026. Around those two lines: revenue of $772M with $176M of Adj. EBITDA, Rentals up ~31% and Zillow Home Loans origination nearly doubled, an audience roughly flat at 2,529M visits and 239M AMUU, and a stock at roughly 130× trailing earnings on a thin, just-turned-positive earnings base.

The bet is long-only on a five-to-ten-year horizon. The market, in my view, overweights agentic-AI disruption relative to Zillow’s marketplace and data position. What would change my mind first is sustained audience decay; second is AI-search taking a fast-rising share of housing queries. But the unit of product is not the audience. It is the basis points of industry transaction value Zillow captures, and the attachment mix that should expand that take. Those two lines lead this letter. Audience, Comscore reach, Rentals and Zillow Home Loans are the support cast.

The spine — monetization intensity × attachment

Start with how hard the marketplace monetizes each dollar of industry transaction value. For Sale revenue divided by industry total transaction value, trailing twelve months, ran 10.1–10.3 bps from FY2024 through 3Q25 — then printed 8.5 at FY2025, 8.7 in 1Q26 and 8.8 in 2Q26. Read the level reset with care: beginning with the fourth quarter of 2025 the TTV denominator comes from NAR-published home counts and prices instead of Zillow’s own estimates. Recasting the TTM ended June 2025 onto NAR moved TTV up roughly 26% and the bps ratio down roughly 20%. The dotted marker on the chart is that methodology boundary — not a continuous like-for-like slope across FY2025. The live question is whether the ratio rebuilds from the high-8s. The last two prints do tick up.

The mechanism that is supposed to rebuild it sits in the right panel: moving connections onto success-fee terms. A connection is a validated lead — consumer contact information delivered to a Premier Agent partner — not a closing. Closing is what triggers the Preferred fee. Preferred / Enhanced connections were 21% of connections at the end of 2024 and reached 49% by Q1’26, through 24, 27, 34 and 44 at the quarter-ends in between. The issuer’s label shifted across those prints (Enhanced Markets vs Enhanced Market experience), and the 2Q26 letter restates the 2024 and 2025 year-ends as “connections in Preferred” at the same 21% / 44% levels while guiding past 75% by end-2026. The series is one period-end percent of connections throughout; era labels stay on the chart. The line stops at Q1’26 — there is no Preferred / Enhanced print for Q2’26. The falsifier link is explicit: if attachment keeps rising and bps do not follow, the mix shift is not pricing power.

A newer, separate metric — integrated experience via Preferred partners — printed 61% of connections at Q2’26. That is a different definition from the Enhanced Markets attachment series. It is labeled here only so the two are not conflated, and it is never merged into the 21→49% line above.

Revenue by engine — For Sale vs Rentals

Zillow’s quarterly revenue stack splits as-filed quarters into Residential, Mortgages and Rentals. Residential plus Mortgages is exactly the issuer’s For Sale grouping — printed as one For Sale line from FY2024 year-end — so the stack reads identically across that disclosure boundary: $465M + $84M = $549M of For Sale in Q2’26, against $209M of Rentals. Full-year anchors: FY2025 For Sale $1,903M and Rentals $630M, versus FY2024 $1,739M and $453M.

Second engines — Rentals and ZHL

Rentals revenue reached $209M in Q2’26 versus $159M a year ago, about +31% — the clearest growth line in the stack. Zillow Home Loans purchase origination hit $2.17B versus $1.12B, nearly doubling year over year. Together they are the answer to a flat audience: more transaction per visit, not more visits. They support the spine rather than lead it — Rentals is still ~27% of quarterly revenue, and ZHL feeds the same attachment thesis. Q4 Rentals bars are the same letter overlays as the engine stack; ZHL stays empty on those residual slots — empty, not zero.

Audience — flat, not broken

Demoted from the lead, and deliberately so: the audience is a falsifier gauge, not the unit of value. Q2’26 visits of 2,529M and 239M average monthly unique users sit just below Q2’25 (2,590M / 243M) — a low-single-digit drift, not a collapse. Q1 was softer at 2,276M / 220M against 2,354M / 227M a year earlier. Two years of quarterly prints show a plateau, not a downtrend break — exactly what the primary falsifier watches.

Definitions

TTV — Total Transaction Value
“TTV is calculated as the number of existing residential homes sold during the relevant period multiplied by the average sales price of existing residential homes sold during the same period.” It is Total Transaction Value for the residential real estate industry — not Zillow’s own closed GMV, and there is no separate company-TTV Key Metric. Source change: before the three months ended Dec 31, 2025 Zillow collected/estimated existing-home sales and ASP; beginning with that quarter the inputs are NAR-published. The TTM ended Jun 30, 2025 recast to NAR was an approximately 26% increase in TTV and a 20% decrease in For Sale revenue per TTV versus the prior Zillow-estimated basis. Q2 2026 Form 10-Q MD&A Key Metrics, accession 0001617640-26-000052
For Sale revenue / TTV (TTM bps)
“We calculate For Sale revenue per TTV as total For Sale revenue for the relevant period divided by the aggregate TTV for the same period.” Trailing-twelve-month basis, in basis points. Cabinet series: 10.1 (FY2024) → 10.2 / 10.3 / 10.1 (1Q–3Q25) → 8.5 (FY2025) → 8.7 (1Q26) → 8.8 (2Q26) — the step into the high-8s overlaps the NAR methodology window, so the slope across 4Q25 straddles two TTV sources. Q2 2026 10-Q (0001617640-26-000052); metric dictionary for_sale_rev_per_ttv_bps_ttm
Connections
“Connections are delivered when consumer contact information is provided to Premier Agent partners.” A connection is a validated lead — not a closing. Under Zillow Preferred, partners “pay a performance advertising fee when a real estate transaction is closed with one of the leads, generally within two years”: closing triggers the fee, not the connection count. Absolute connection count N and $/connection are not disclosed (GAP). Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, accession 0001617640-26-000052; 10-K 0001617640-26-000015 (fee mechanics)
Rev / connection uplift (relative)
Issuer relative print only: Preferred “generating 23% more revenue per connection than our legacy advertising model did.” In 2025, “23% higher revenue per connection compared to our legacy advertising model”; by end of 2026 the company expects “35% more revenue per connection.” Methodology (issuer): total Preferred revenue + revenue from Preferred agent partners using Follow Up Boss and Showcase + revenue from integrated transactions where consumers use Zillow Home Loans, versus legacy advertising (+ FUB/Showcase on the legacy side). Absolute $/connection remains a GAP. 2Q26 letter 0001617640-26-000050 Ex99.2; desk dig z-unit-economics-derived-2026-09-20
Zillow Preferred vs Enhanced Markets
Preferred is the pay-for-performance Premier Agent pricing product; Enhanced Markets are the geographies “where the integrated housing super app experience is most fully realized” (4Q24 letter). The % of connections series prints 21 (Q4’24) → 24 → 27 → 34 → 44 (Q4’25) → 49 (Q1’26) under era labels (“Enhanced Markets” / “Enhanced Market experience”). The 2Q26 letter restates YE2024 / YE2025 as “connections in Preferred” at 21% / 44% — the same levels — and guides to more than 75% of connections in Preferred by end of 2026. Preferred is the product umbrella for the attachment goal; Enhanced Markets remains the geo / experience framing. Shareholder letters 0001617640-25-000015 (4Q24), 0001617640-26-000012 (4Q25), 0001617640-26-000038 (1Q26); desk dig z-preferred-enhanced-dig-2026-09-17

Visits
A group of interactions on the Zillow, Trulia, and StreetEasy apps and sites. Trulia visits end after 30 minutes of inactivity; Zillow and StreetEasy visits end after 30 minutes of inactivity or at midnight. Measured with an internal tool for Zillow / StreetEasy and Adobe Analytics for Trulia. FY rows store annual visit totals (FY2025: 9,593M) and are never mixed with the quarterly series. Q2 2026 10-Q Key Metrics, accession 0001617640-26-000052
AMUU — average monthly unique users
Unique users are counted as the first access per mobile app / web browser / device / website in a calendar month — one consumer on two browsers counts twice. AMUU averages that monthly count across the period. Issuer count rules; not a Comscore measure. Q2 2026 10-Q Key Metrics, accession 0001617640-26-000052
