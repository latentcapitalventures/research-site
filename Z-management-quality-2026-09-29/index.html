The short version

After the August 2024 handoff from founder Rich Barton to long-time operator Jeremy Wacksman, is Zillow underwritable as a stewardship franchise — board process, capital returns, internal bench — or still a key-person, dual-class founder-control bet?

Zillow Desk · Latent capital · dig D · as of 2026-09-29

Verdict

CEO seat already moved once — Wacksman (at Z since 2009; COO → CEO Aug 2024) runs day-to-day; Barton and Frink sit as Co-Executive Chairmen.

Founders still carry the vote (~36% + ~24%; ~61% with the director/officer group via Class B). Buybacks live; diluted shares mid-250Ms → ~228M by Q2’26.

Process is visible; founder voting power remains the load-bearing underwrite. OPEN.

Chief executive and chief financial officer

Wacksman. Fifteen years inside the house before the chair: marketing, then brand, then Offers, then president, then chief operating officer, then chief executive. The proxy frames the co-chair structure as oversight plus founder product depth while the chief executive runs the day. Beneficial ownership is almost entirely Class C options and restricted units. Voting power is under 1%.

Hofmann. Finance seat since May 2023, after corporate development and strategy, with a Goldman capital-markets background. Same pattern: Class C equity awards, no Class B vote.

Bench and succession

The chief-executive transition already happened, and it was internal. The nominating and governance committee’s printed duties include management succession planning. What is not printed: a named heir after Wacksman, or a named emergency chief executive. Bench depth shows in the roles — chief operating officer, chief technology officer, industry lead — not in a named succession tree.

Capital allocation and skin in the game

Diluted weighted-average shares peaked around 256 million in 2025, then stepped to 239.6 million in the first quarter of 2026 and 227.9 million in the second. The May 2025 authorization and the full-year and fourth-quarter repurchase prints are marked on that path as dates. They are not a claimed cause of the next print.

Full-year 2025 cash buybacks were $670 million. The fourth quarter alone was $232 million for 3.4 million shares. In the first quarter of 2026 the letter printed $127 million of adjusted free cash flow, up 44% from a year earlier. That is one point. It is not yet a multi-year series of cash returned against free cash flow.

The March 24, 2026 proxy is the skin table. Founders dominate the vote. Caledonia and Vanguard lead the Class A book above 5%. Executive economic exposure sits heavily in Class C awards. The board has not adopted formal stock-ownership guidelines for the named executive officers. Barton LLC has 450,000 Class C shares pledged. That is a disclosed footnote, not a grade.
