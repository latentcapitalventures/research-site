1. What Zillow is and how it makes money

Zillow Group runs consumer brands — Zillow, Trulia, StreetEasy, HotPads — that aggregate demand for homes and rentals. Money shows up in three reportable engines:

1. Residential — Premier Agent / Preferred-style connection advertising and related for-sale marketplace products. 2. Mortgages — including Zillow Home Loans (ZHL) purchase origination economics. 3. Rentals — multifamily and rental marketplace revenue.

For Sale = Residential + Mortgages when the letter prints that subtotal. Rentals stands alone. Adjusted EBITDA is the non-GAAP operating pulse management highlights alongside GAAP NI.

Q4 letter spine (FACT, locked): Resid / Mort / Rentals $349M / $22M / $93M (Q4’23, accession `0001617640-24-000012`) → $387M / $41M / $116M (Q4’24, `0001617640-25-000015`) → $418M / $57M / $168M (Q4’25, `0001617640-26-000012`). Prefer these letter prints over any residual invention.

By Q2’26 the company printed $772M total revenue, $549M For Sale, $209M Rentals, Adj. EBITDA $176M, and a slight GAAP loss (−$4M). Cash & investments were $572M. The P&L is no longer a science project; the question is mix quality and take-rate, not survival.

2. Growth engine vs legacy core

Think of the stack as two stories sharing one front door.

Legacy core — For Sale marketplace. Still the largest dollar engine (FY2025 For Sale $1,903M). Inside it, the quality of monetization is migrating from share-of-voice / legacy ads toward Preferred (success-fee) and Enhanced-market connection mix. That migration is the growth story inside the core — not a separate segment line.

Growth engines — Rentals + ZHL attach. Rentals is the clear second locomotive: Q2’26 $209M vs Q2’25 $159M. ZHL purchase origination is the attach flywheel on the same buyers: Q2’26 $2.174B vs Q2’25 $1.116B; Q4 letter prints alone run $487M → $923M → $1.5B across Q4’23–Q4’25.

The front door that feeds both engines is still enormous. Issuer-quoted Comscore (not licensed) puts Zillow Group at ~70% of the real-estate audience in both Q1’26 and Q2’26 (`0001617640-26-000038`, `0001617640-26-000050`). That is the thesis’s “network / attention moat” print — and also the first place a falsifier would show up.

Definitions

TTV — Total Transaction Value
“TTV is calculated as the number of existing residential homes sold during the relevant period multiplied by the average sales price of existing residential homes sold during the same period.” It is Total Transaction Value for the residential real estate industry — not Zillow’s own closed GMV, and there is no separate company-TTV Key Metric. Source change: before the three months ended Dec 31, 2025 Zillow collected/estimated existing-home sales and ASP; beginning with that quarter the inputs are NAR-published. The TTM ended Jun 30, 2025 recast to NAR was an approximately 26% increase in TTV and a 20% decrease in For Sale revenue per TTV versus the prior Zillow-estimated basis. Q2 2026 Form 10-Q MD&A Key Metrics, accession 0001617640-26-000052
For Sale revenue / TTV (TTM bps)
“We calculate For Sale revenue per TTV as total For Sale revenue for the relevant period divided by the aggregate TTV for the same period.” Trailing-twelve-month basis, in basis points. Cabinet series: 10.1 (FY2024) → 10.2 / 10.3 / 10.1 (1Q–3Q25) → 8.5 (FY2025) → 8.7 (1Q26) → 8.8 (2Q26) — the step into the high-8s overlaps the NAR methodology window, so the slope across 4Q25 straddles two TTV sources. Q2 2026 10-Q (0001617640-26-000052); metric dictionary for_sale_rev_per_ttv_bps_ttm
Connections
“Connections are delivered when consumer contact information is provided to Premier Agent partners.” A connection is a validated lead — not a closing. Under Zillow Preferred, partners “pay a performance advertising fee when a real estate transaction is closed with one of the leads, generally within two years”: closing triggers the fee, not the connection count. Absolute connection count N and $/connection are not disclosed (GAP). Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, accession 0001617640-26-000052; 10-K 0001617640-26-000015 (fee mechanics)
Rev / connection uplift (relative)
Issuer relative print only: Preferred “generating 23% more revenue per connection than our legacy advertising model did.” In 2025, “23% higher revenue per connection compared to our legacy advertising model”; by end of 2026 the company expects “35% more revenue per connection.” Methodology (issuer): total Preferred revenue + revenue from Preferred agent partners using Follow Up Boss and Showcase + revenue from integrated transactions where consumers use Zillow Home Loans, versus legacy advertising (+ FUB/Showcase on the legacy side). Absolute $/connection remains a GAP. 2Q26 letter 0001617640-26-000050 Ex99.2; desk dig z-unit-economics-derived-2026-09-20
Zillow Preferred vs Enhanced Markets
Preferred is the pay-for-performance Premier Agent pricing product; Enhanced Markets are the geographies “where the integrated housing super app experience is most fully realized” (4Q24 letter). The % of connections series prints 21 (Q4’24) → 24 → 27 → 34 → 44 (Q4’25) → 49 (Q1’26) under era labels (“Enhanced Markets” / “Enhanced Market experience”). The 2Q26 letter restates YE2024 / YE2025 as “connections in Preferred” at 21% / 44% — the same levels — and guides to more than 75% of connections in Preferred by end of 2026. Preferred is the product umbrella for the attachment goal; Enhanced Markets remains the geo / experience framing. Shareholder letters 0001617640-25-000015 (4Q24), 0001617640-26-000012 (4Q25), 0001617640-26-000038 (1Q26); desk dig z-preferred-enhanced-dig-2026-09-17
Integrated experience % of connections (61%)
“…this integrated experience through our Preferred partners, and it now accounts for 61% of our connections across Zillow.” A separate field (integrated_experience_pct_connections, 61 at 2026-06-30 only). The issuer did not put 61% into the Enhanced Markets / “connections in Preferred” series table, so the two are never merged unless an issuer table equates them. 2Q26 letter 0001617640-26-000050 Ex99.2
Visits
A group of interactions on the Zillow, Trulia, and StreetEasy apps and sites. Trulia visits end after 30 minutes of inactivity; Zillow and StreetEasy visits end after 30 minutes of inactivity or at midnight. Measured with an internal tool for Zillow / StreetEasy and Adobe Analytics for Trulia. FY rows store annual visit totals (FY2025: 9,593M) and are never mixed with the quarterly series. Q2 2026 10-Q Key Metrics, accession 0001617640-26-000052
AMUU — average monthly unique users
Unique users are counted as the first access per mobile app / web browser / device / website in a calendar month — one consumer on two browsers counts twice. AMUU averages that monthly count across the period. Issuer count rules; not a Comscore measure. Q2 2026 10-Q Key Metrics, accession 0001617640-26-000052
Comscore audience share (issuer-quoted)
Zillow Group average monthly unique visitors ÷ Comscore “real estate” category unique visitors, as quoted in issuer materials: 70% at both 2026-03-31 and 2026-06-30, with group Comscore MUV of 127M cited once at 1Q26. Issuer-quoted, not a licensed Comscore series — two dated prints are a labeled table, never a share trend. The only other print anywhere is the 4Q24 investor deck’s 66% as of December 2024 — a dated historical footnote, never a live read. facts_external + metric dictionary; accessions 0001617640-26-000038 (1Q26), 0001617640-26-000050 (2Q26)
Method & definitional notes
  • FY annual visit totals are labeled annual and excluded from quarterly charts (FY2025 9,593M vs quarterly ~2.2–2.6B).
  • pe_ttm prints only where trailing-four-quarter net income is complete and positive (1Q26, 2Q26); sparse labeled prints, never connected.
  • pe_fy is non-null only at FY2025 (≈753.90) and sits on thin earnings — FY2025 net income was only +$23M; it is a labeled print, not a trend point.
  • Weekly trailing PE uses complete ISO weeks only (VWAP of daily closes); positive-TTM coverage starts 2026-W14; latest ok week 2026-W37 ≈ 132.86.
  • TTV methodology: TTV (industry existing-home $ volume = homes sold × ASP) flipped from Zillow-estimated inputs to NAR-published beginning with the three months ended 2025-12-31; the TTM ended 2025-06-30 recast to NAR was ≈ +26% TTV and ≈ −20% For Sale revenue per TTV — the bps slope across 4Q25 straddles two TTV sources.
  • Quarantine: pre-FY2022 Adj. EBITDA cells in the cabinet are scale-suspect (unit errors) and stay nulled here pending MD&A re-extract; 2021 Zillow Home Loans purchase-origination cells are unit-suspect versus 2022+ prints and stay nulled pending exhibit audit.
  • FY2022 is continuing-ops on the YE / restated basis only (Q4 revenue $435M per the 10-K Item 7 quarterly-results table, accession 0001617640-23-000010; restated Q1–Q3 $536M / $504M / $483M → FY $1,958M). The as-filed Homes-era Q1 $4.257B is never cited unlabeled, and the refused −$3.791B Q4 revenue residual is gone from the store and never cited.
  • Q2’26 pe_ttm uses the 2026-09-17 asof close ($30.11 → ~124.8×), not the quarter-end close — distinct from weekly W37 ~132.86×.
  • Street Normalized FY1/FY2 overlays (~14.44× / ~11.49×) are from desk note 2026-09-17 only — eps_estimate.csv has no Z rows as of 2026-09-21; blank NTM stays blank.
  • Cash & investments mirrors cash & equivalents in the cabinet; short-term investments are null throughout.
  • Preferred / Enhanced % of connections is an issuer period-end print; the program label shifted between “Enhanced Markets” and “Enhanced Market experience” across prints, and the 2024-12-31 / 2025-12-31 reads sit on the cabinet’s FY rows.
  • Integrated experience % of connections (61% at 2026-06-30) is a separate metric (integrated_experience_pct_connections) — never merged into the Preferred / Enhanced series.
  • Comscore audience share is issuer-quoted (Zillow-cited), not a licensed Comscore series: 70% at both 2026-03-31 and 2026-06-30, group MUV cited once — two prints are a labeled table, never a share trend. The 4Q24 investor-deck 66% (as of Dec 2024) is a dated historical footnote only, never a live read.
  • Unit economics: absolute connection count N and $/connection are GAP — never FACT. For Sale × Preferred% ≠ Preferred revenue (connection mix, not revenue share). Sensitivity $/conn ceilings are ASSUMPTION on N (FY2025 For Sale $1,903M as crude upper-bound proxy).
  • Non-additive: FY annual visit totals (FY2023 9,977M / FY2024 9,308M / FY2025 9,593M) are not comparable to quarterly visits (~2.2–2.6B) — never summed or charted on one axis.
