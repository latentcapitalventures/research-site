Booking Holdings — peer benchmark

BKNG-peer-benchmark-2026-10-06 · USD · series 2026-10-07T01:45:00Z · built 2026-10-07 02:18 UTC

The short version

Travelers pay Booking when they book a stay or other travel through its brands. Expedia, Airbnb, and Trip.com take the same kind of booking — hotel, home, or package — with different inventory and geography weight. Expedia is the closest large OTA peer on stays and packages. Airbnb is the homes and direct-to-guest alternative. Trip.com is the major international OTA with Asia weight, reporting in CNY on Form 20-F.

Booking is the largest USD revenue print in the set. Trip.com stays blank on the dollar charts and has its own CNY panel.

Operating income is the honest profit line. Margin is that line divided by revenue (DERIVED). Trip.com’s CNY margin sits on the percent chart because the ratio is currency-neutral.
