The short version

Booking Holdings is a global online travel company. Travelers use Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, KAYAK, and OpenTable to find and book stays and other travel. Booking earns commissions and related fees when those bookings happen — sometimes collecting payment itself (merchant), sometimes letting the supplier collect (agency) — plus advertising and metasearch fees.

This pack puts the income and valuation scoreboard on the table: as-reported revenue, operating income, net income, and EPS; revenue and net-income growth; and trailing weekly PE. Room nights, gross bookings, and take rate live on the separate unit-econ dig — they are not duplicated here.

Trailing twelve-month diluted EPS is about $9.36 through the quarter ended June 30, 2026. For the complete week of September 14–20, 2026 (2026-W38), trailing PE was about 18.3× on a weekly volume-weighted average price of about $171. Forward PE stays blank: there is no locked street or guidance print.

How it makes money

The company sits between travelers and hotels, airlines, restaurants, and other travel suppliers. It takes a cut when a booking is completed and also earns advertising and metasearch fees. Brand-by-brand profit is not a chart here. Merchant versus agency mix and booking scale (room nights, GBV, take rate) are locked on the unit-econ dig.

Growth — revenue and earnings

After the pandemic rebound, revenue has kept compounding. Full-year 2025 revenue was $26.9 billion, up about 13% from 2024. Operating income rose with it, to $8.83 billion (up about 17%). Net income did not: it fell about 8% to $5.40 billion. That gap between a stronger operating line and a weaker bottom line is the clearest scoreboard fact on the annual series.

The latest reported quarter is second-quarter 2026: revenue $7.35 billion, operating income $2.50 billion, net income $1.95 billion, diluted EPS $2.53 (post 25-for-1 split). Versus the same quarter a year earlier, revenue was up about 8% and net income up about 118% — a wide year-over-year swing that owes as much to the prior-year base as to run-rate. Versus the prior quarter, revenue was up about 33% and net income about 80% (seasonal).
